Delete your LOOP account

App: LOOP — Private world for two (package com.loopworld.app) · Last updated: 12 November 2026

Delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Open LOOP and sign in.
  2. Tap Us at the bottom.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Can't open the app?

Email rutujaainfo@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my LOOP account. We delete the account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address, display name and sign-in).
  • Everything you sent: notes, voice tapes, photos, letters, bouquets and radio dedications, including the stored files.
  • Your space memberships, settings, push notification token and subscription record.

What is kept

  • Nothing about you is kept in LOOP after deletion. Backups roll off automatically within about 30 days.
  • Things your person already received are deleted together with the items, so they disappear from their app too.
  • Google Play keeps its own purchase records. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Subscriptions.

Delete only some data

You can use Us → Leave this space to remove one private space and all items in it without deleting your account.

See the LOOP privacy policy.
