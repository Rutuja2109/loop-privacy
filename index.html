LOOP — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 · Contact: rutujaainfo@gmail.com

LOOP is a private space for two people (a partner, friend, sibling or family member). This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it. We collect only what the app needs to work. We do not show ads, we do not sell your data, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, a password (stored hashed by our sign-in provider, never readable by us) and the display name you choose.
  • What you send inside a private space: handwritten notes, voice tapes (audio), photos, letters, bouquets, radio dedications, your nickname and theme, and which items you have opened or favourited.
  • Space membership: which private spaces you belong to and who the other person is, so items reach the right person.
  • Push notification token: an identifier from Firebase Cloud Messaging for your phone, used only to tell you when something new arrives.
  • Subscription status: if you buy LOOP Together, we record the plan, status and expiry date, and a purchase token from Google Play to confirm it is real. We never see your card or UPI details; Google Play handles payment.
  • Settings: for example whether you and your person both allowed notes to appear on each other's home screen.

Who can see it

Only the two members of a private space can read what is sent in it. This is enforced by access rules on our database and file storage. Other spaces you belong to are separate and never mixed. Our service providers process data on our behalf only to run LOOP: Supabase (accounts, database and file storage), Google Firebase (push notifications) and Google Play (purchases). Handwriting you draw while both of you have the home-screen screen open is relayed live between your two phones through Supabase and is not stored once you leave it, unless you press Send.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone: only while you hold REC to record a voice tape. Never in the background.
  • Photos / camera: only when you choose a picture to share, through Android's own picker. LOOP does not read your gallery.
  • Notifications: to tell you when your person sends something.
  • Internet: to sync with your person and to stream radio.
  • Home-screen widgets: the photo and Screen note widgets show what your person sent. The Screen note widget works only when both of you switch it on, and either of you can switch it off at any time.

Third parties you may contact

When you play a radio station, your device connects directly to that station's server (for example Radio Paradise or SomaFM), which can see your IP address, as with any radio app. Station search queries go to the public Radio Browser directory. We do not control those services.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. If you leave a space, the items in that space are deleted. If you delete your account, your profile, subscription record and everything you sent are permanently deleted. Backups roll off automatically shortly afterwards.

Your choices and deleting your data

In the app, open Us → Delete my account to permanently delete your account and everything you sent. You can also use Leave this space to remove a single space, or switch off home-screen notes at any time. If you cannot open the app, email rutujaainfo@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete your account and data. Cancelling a subscription is done in Google Play → Subscriptions; deleting your LOOP account does not cancel it by itself.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Files are stored privately and are readable only by the members of the space. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

LOOP is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
